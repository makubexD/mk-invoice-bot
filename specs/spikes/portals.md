## La Leña's site in detail (2026-11-13, phase `fetch` step 3)

`config/portals/facturactiva-la-lena.yaml` now drives a lookup on its own; a throwaway driver read only that file and got "found" for a real October invoice and "not found" for a made-up número.

- **Form:** Ant Design. The issuer RUC is a hidden, prefilled field. `tipo` is an Ant select: click `#tipoComprobante`, then the option whose `title` is the site's label (`Factura`, `Boleta de venta`, ...). `serie` and `correlativo` are plain inputs (`correlativo` takes the número without leading zeros). `montoTotal` takes the total with two decimals.
- **Date:** `#fecha` is a read-only Ant date picker, so it can't be typed. Clicking it opens the current month; the previous-month button steps back until the cell `td[title='YYYY-MM-DD']` shows.
- **Found:** the page moves to a result URL with buttons `Descargar PDF`, `Descargar XML`, `Descargar CDR` and `Imprimir Documento`. The PDF and XML buttons start real downloads (`expect_download`). The downloaded XML matched the invoice (R-5.6).
- **Not found:** the form stays, and an Ant toast (`.ant-message-notice`) says "No se encontró un comprobante que coincida con los datos ingresados" within 1 s.
- **New portal-file keys this needs:** `tipo_labels`, and two field kinds besides plain text and `select`: `pick` (click the field, then an option) and `calendar` (`cell` and `prev` selectors).
